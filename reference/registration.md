# Auckland Council Animal Management dog registration data

Data compiled by Auckland Council's Animal Management division relating
to registered dogs in Auckland for financial years 2021-2025.

## Usage

``` r
registration
```

## Format

Data frame with columns

- sheet_name:

  Excel sheet from which the row originates in the raw workbook.

- animal_id:

  Animal ID number.

- registration_latest_year:

  Latest year the animal held a valid registration.

- animal_breed_description:

  Primary breed.

- animal_breed2_description:

  Secondary breed.

- animal_age:

  Age of animal.

- classification:

  Current classification under the Dog Control Act.

- suburb_name:

  Suburb dog is registered in.

- owner_registration_class:

  Whether the owner uses a SuperGold discount or has a RDOL.

- owner_classification_desc:

  Whether the owner is subject to a classification under the Dog Control
  Act.

- animal_registration_group:

  Animal registration group.

- animal_gender_description:

  Sex of the animal.

- animal_desexed:

  Whether or not the animal is desexed.

- address_area:

  Region of Auckland dog is registered in.

- dob:

  Animal's date of birth.
