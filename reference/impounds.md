# Auckland Council Animal Management dog impound data

Data compiled by Auckland Council's Animal Management division relating
to impounded dogs in Auckland for financial years 2022-2024.

## Usage

``` r
impounds
```

## Format

Data frame with columns

- docket_number:

  Docket number assigned to impound event.

- shelter:

  Shelter receiving impounded animal.

- team:

  Animal Management team responsible for impound.

- impound_date:

  Date of impound.

- impound_reason:

  Reason for impound.

- exit_date:

  Date impounded animal left the shelter.

- status:

  Reason for departure.

- rfs_number:

  Corresponding Request for Service number.

- street_name:

  Street animal was uplifted from.

- suburb:

  Suburb animal was uplifted from.

- s_57_type:

  Reason for impound, if impounded under Section 57 of the Dog Control
  Act.

- severity:

  Severity of attack.

- primary_breed:

  Primary dog breed.

- secondary_breed:

  Secondary dog breed.

- sex:

  Sex of the animal.

- desexed:

  Desexed status.

- approx_age:

  Approximate age of the animal.

- dog_name:

  Name of dog.

- dog_id:

  Dog ID number.

- classified:

  Whether or not the dog has a current classification under the Dog
  Control Act.

- current_classification:

  Current classification under the Dog Control Act.

- classification_required:

  Whether or not a new classification is required as a result of
  circumstances leading to impounding.

- new_classification:

  Whether or not a new classification has been issued.

- new_classification_issued:

  New classification issued as a result of circumstances leading to
  impounding.

- registered:

  Whether or not dog is registered.

- new_reg_issued:

  Whether or not a new registration has been issued for the dog.

- microchipped:

  Whether or not the dog is microchipped.

- microchip_number:

  Microchip ID number.

- new_microchip_issued:

  Whether or not a new microchip ID number has been issued.

- known_dog:

  Whether or not the dog is 'known' to Animal Management.

- euthanasia_reason:

  Reason for euthanasia.

- days_in_shelter:

  Number of days in the shelter.

## Source
